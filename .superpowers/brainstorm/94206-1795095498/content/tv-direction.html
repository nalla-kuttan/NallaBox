Which mood should the TV screen have?

Same lobby, three looks. The TV is what the whole room stares at all night; the phones and host app will follow whichever you pick. I'd pick A: dark is calmer in a dim living room, and coral pops hardest on Night Ink.

A

Night stage

Night Ink background, coral for the room code and the leader. Easiest on the eyes in a dim room; feels like a show.

B

Paper

Warm off-white, ink text, coral saved for moments. Friendly and daytime; glares more on a big TV at night.

C

Coral blast

The whole screen is the brand colour. Loudest and most ownable; tiring over a two-hour session and leaves little room for game colours.
